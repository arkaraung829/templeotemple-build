Executive Summary

A single marketplace for everything a temple and a devotee need

TempleOTemple brings every temple-related product and service into one trusted app — so devotees order and book in one place, vendors gain customers and automatic payments, and you earn a commission on every transaction.

The platform is built on a proven, battle-tested marketplace foundation already designed for India — so we launch faster, at lower cost, and with lower risk than building from scratch.

Your Business Model

You earn on every transaction — automatically

📊

Commission per order

A % of every order/booking, set by you per category (typical 2%–15%).

⚡

Automatic settlement

Customer pays in full; your commission is retained and the vendor is auto-paid — no manual accounting.

🧾

GST compliant

Tax invoices, GSTIN/PAN on vendor records, proper tax breakup.

Future revenue levers (optional)

Vendor premium subscriptions · promoted listings · featured placement during festivals.

A festival day — in real life

Aadi month, 6 AM. Priya opens TempleOTemple, books a Ganapathi Homam for her new home, and adds an archanai kit and 25 return gifts — paying ₹5,200 by UPI in one tap.

Instantly: Priest Raman gets a booking alert and confirms the 6 AM slot · Sri Flowers gets the kit order and packs it for same-day delivery · the gift vendor ships the return gifts.

For Bala: ₹5,200 flows through Razorpay — each vendor is auto-paid their share, and Bala keeps ~₹480 commission from this one order. On a busy festival day with 400 such orders, that's ~₹1.9 lakh in a single day.

Built for Reality

How we handle the hard parts

The operational truths that make a temple marketplace actually work — not just look good.

🌸

Perishable flowers

Same-day & scheduled delivery windows, local vendors matched by area, and festival cut-off times.

🔱

Priest no-shows

Confirmed bookings, reminders, verified priests, and a backup-priest fallback so a pooja never fails.

✅

Trust & quality

KYC-verified vendors, ratings & reviews, and admin approval before anyone can sell.

↩️

Refunds & disputes

Clear per-category cancellation rules; admin refunds via the gateway; money held safely until delivery.

💵

Cash on Delivery

Optional COD for tier-2/3 towns where digital trust is still growing — reconciliation built in.

📶

Low-tech vendors

A simple Tamil seller app, hands-on onboarding, and clear order alerts so any shop can join.
